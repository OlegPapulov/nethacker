## Why it lost

The bot loses because it stops at low experience levels and, in the games that get
the furthest, it kills itself trading melee with tougher monsters. Most seeds end
before experience level 10. In the long seeds the wizard reaches experience level
10 or 11 and then fights a rothe or a dwarf lord blow for blow until it dies. The
wizard's only real attack is the force bolt, and the bolt currently refuses any
monster farther than eight squares, so a tough monster that first appears beyond
eight tiles has to be walked up to and met in melee. Starvation also ends a long
game: on an exhausted first level the wizard faints at full hit points and the
rescue that would move it to dungeon level 2 fires too late to reach the stairs.
