The wizard's plan is to stay on the first floors of the Dungeons of Doom and
farm experience until level 12, because that is where the score is. What ends
those games is a fight the wizard cannot win. Its melee is a quarterstaff and
it only considers retreating at 8 hit points (16 against an insect), so it
keeps trading blows with a monster that out-damages it. The record says so in
hit points: seed 0 dies to a rothe with 61 of 75 still standing, seed 1 to a
dwarf lord with 43 of 98, both on dungeon level 1, both well past the point
where a quarterstaff could have won.

Four games leave those floors and die further down. Seed 13 walks onto a hole
on level 2, falls to level 4, looks around levels 4 and 5 for 3500 turns and is
killed by a dwarf lord on level 3. Seed 9 is killed by an invisible Mordor orc
on level 3, seed 11 by a pony on level 4, seed 7 is poisoned by a rotted rothe
corpse on level 3. Nothing on those floors is worth more than the floor the
wizard already farms safely.

Three games are over almost before they start: seeds 4, 10 and 12 faint from
hunger on level 2 within 6000 turns and are killed by a goblin, a kitten and a
kobold lord. The rest end to what the first floors hold -- bolts of fire and
cold from traps the wizard never saw (seeds 2 and 14), a potion of acid that
was carried unprotected and shattered (seed 8), and ants that take a turn
before the wizard does (seeds 3, 5 and 6).

## What is the problem

Progress is the highest milestone a game reaches, so a death only costs the
levels the wizard never reached. Eight games stop at experience 8 to 11, one
step short of the next scoring band.
