## Why it stopped

The parent batch is not in this note. The judge plays the 15 seeds after you exit.

In local play the bot farms the first Doom level to raise its experience. Food runs
out, hunger falls to fainting, and the wizard is helpless for those turns. Most games
end there: a first-floor melee monster kills the fainting wizard at experience level
5 to 7. The food latch to dungeon level 2 fires only once the wizard is already
fainting and no edible corpse is in reach, which is usually too late. The food prayer
that keeps the wizard alive is asked from that same fainting state, so it is the last
resort rather than a fix.
