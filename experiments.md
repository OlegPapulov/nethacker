## The change

Rewrite `Agent.bfs` (in `autoascend/agent.py`).

`bfs` decides which tiles the wizard may walk on. Outside a fifty-turn
"escape window" it already removes every trap from the walkable set, so the
wizard normally never steps on a trap. Inside that window -- opened when the
bot has searched the same tile more than four hundred times and is otherwise
stuck -- *every* trap becomes walkable, including the ones that do not merely
hurt the wizard but **move it to another floor**: a hole or a trap door drops
it one to three levels down, and a magic trap, a teleportation trap or a level
teleporter flings it away. The first floors are the only place this character
levels safely; a wizard dropped to depth three through five lands among
monsters it cannot beat and dies there.

The rewrite keeps the escape window for every harmful-but-recoverable trap
(web, bear trap, pit, land mine, ...) so a genuine stall is still escapable,
but never lets the wizard walk onto `S_hole`, `S_trap_door`, `S_fire_trap`,
`S_magic_trap`, `S_teleportation_trap` or `S_level_teleporter` -- even while
that window is open. The wizard therefore stays on the floor it is farming.
