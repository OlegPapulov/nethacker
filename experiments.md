Rewrite the `bfs` method of `Agent` (`autoascend/agent.py`).

The parent `bfs` drops traps out of the walkable mask only while the
search-stall window is shut:

    if self._last_turn - self._allow_walking_through_traps_turn > 50:
        walkable &= ~utils.isin(level.objects, G.TRAPS)

Once the wizard is stuck on a fully explored floor (`search_diff > 400`, the
usual state while farming dungeon level 1) that window is open, so every known
trap becomes a legal tile on the walk line. On the first floors that includes a
bolt trap, which throws fire or cold and can kill the wizard in one hit; seeds 2,
8 and 14 all end this way on dungeon level 1 while farming.

The rewrite keeps a fire trap or a magic trap out of the walkable mask on every
call, whether or not the search-stall window is open:

    walkable &= ~utils.isin(level.objects, G.LETHAL_TRAPS)

with `G.LETHAL_TRAPS` the two bolt traps (`S_fire_trap`, `S_magic_trap`). The
window still reopens every other trap, so it keeps breaking the stalls it was
added for; the two bolt traps simply stop being legal path tiles, so the wizard
routes around them instead of being walked onto one. This changes no listed
action: it leaves the force bolt, the farm stay, the food latch, the corpse
reach, the melee bonus, the zaps, the prayers and the chosen suit untouched.
