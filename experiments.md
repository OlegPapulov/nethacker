## Proposal

Rewrite one function: `get_potential_wand_usages` in
`autoascend/combat/fight_heur.py`.

The wizard aims ray wands (fire, cold, lightning, death, magic missile) by
simulating where the ray goes. A ray is not a beam: it does not stop on the
first monster or door, it passes through and reflects off walls. The simulation
already walks every square the ray reaches and records the wizard's own square
as `'self'`, but the current function only *discounts* that self-hit
(`priority -= p * 30`). Against a monster the agent calls dangerous the reward
is `p * 25` counted once per pass, so a ray that bounces back over the wizard
can still be worth more than the discount and be chosen. That is the game's own
warning made real: "A dangerous early use is ... a wand aimed at yourself."
Two judge seeds ended exactly that way ("killed by a bolt of fire" and "killed
by a bolt of cold") on depth 1 at only Xp:8.

The rewrite refuses the direction outright: if any simulated hit is `'self'`,
the wand usage for that direction is not offered at all. It does not touch the
peaceful/pet discount (the rules say stopping a zap on a peaceful monster
lowers the score), it does not change which wands are usable, and it does not
change the beam spell (`force bolt`), which cannot reflect. Only self-inflicted
ray deaths are removed.

## Why this raises the score

Progress is the maximum experience level reached. The change can only remove an
action that has a chance to injure the wizard, so it either saves the two
"bolt" seeds and lets them grind further, or leaves the run byte-identical.
It does not gate any XP-earning action behind a stricter condition; it only
drops a zap that the ray simulation itself says can reach the wizard.
